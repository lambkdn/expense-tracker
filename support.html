Support

Expense Tracker: Ledger · Version 1.0.0

Need help or found a bug? Email vovanlam89@gmail.com and include your iOS version and a short description of what happened. We usually reply within two business days.

How do I add a transaction?

Tap the + button, pick a category, type the amount, then save. To log an older purchase, use the date arrows to move the date back before saving. You can also switch between income and expense with the toggle at the top of the form.

How do I unlock Premium insights?

Open the Store tab, buy the Basic Pack 1 credit pack once, then spend 50 credits to unlock Premium insights. The unlock is permanent and stored on your device. There is no subscription and nothing renews.

I bought credits but they did not appear.

Make sure you are online, then reopen the Store tab. Apple replays any unfinished purchase the next time the app starts, so the credits should arrive automatically. If they still do not show up, email us with your Apple purchase receipt.

Do I need an account?

No. Expense Tracker has no sign-up, no login, and no cloud sync. Everything is stored locally on your iPhone.

How do I delete my data?

Open Settings inside the app and use the reset option, or simply delete the app from your device. Read more in the Privacy Policy.

Does the app work offline?

Yes, entirely. The only feature that needs a connection is the App Store purchase itself.
