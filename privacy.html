Privacy Policy

Expense Tracker: Ledger · Last updated 6 November 2026

Short version: Expense Tracker does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, and no server. Everything you type stays on your own device.

1. Who we are

Expense Tracker: Ledger ("the app") is an iOS application developed and published by Vo Van Lam ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app.

2. Information we collect

We collect nothing. The app has no user accounts, no login, no advertising identifiers, no crash reporting, and no third-party analytics SDKs. We do not have a server that receives your data, so there is nothing for us to store or share.

3. Information you enter

Transactions, categories, notes, amounts, settings and your currency preference are stored locally in a database on your iPhone. They are used only to display the app's screens to you. They are never uploaded, backed up to us, or shared with anyone.

Note that if you have iCloud Backup enabled on your device, Apple may include the app's local data in your device backup. That backup is controlled entirely by you through your Apple ID settings and is governed by Apple's privacy policy, not ours.

4. In-app purchases

The app offers a one-time consumable credit pack ("Basic Pack 1") that unlocks Premium insights. All payments are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your name, payment card, or billing address. To deliver your credits, the app reads the purchase receipt that Apple provides on your device; that receipt is verified locally and is not sent anywhere.

5. Third parties

The only third party involved is Apple, which distributes the app and processes payments under Apple's Privacy Policy. The app contains no ads, no social media integrations, and no external tracking.

6. Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children. Because no data leaves the device, no personal information from a child can reach us.

7. Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device for as long as you keep the app installed. You can erase everything at any time:

  • Open Settings inside the app and choose the option to reset or delete your data.
  • Or delete the app from your device, which removes its local database with it.

Since we never receive your data, there is nothing for you to request from us and nothing for us to delete on our side.

8. Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and CCPA give you rights to access, correct, or delete personal data held about you. We hold no personal data about you at all, which means these rights are satisfied by design: the only copy of your information is the one on your own device, under your control.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date at the top. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
